The choice between a frozen and a fine-tuned vision–language model for plant-disease imaging is usually made without a controlled comparison of adaptation protocols. We compare two generic CLIP checkpoints (DFN-CLIP, LAION-2B; both ViT-L/14) with a fine-tuned ResNet-18 across zero-shot, few-shot (K in {1,2,5,10}, 15 seeds) and full-data regimes. The ordering is set by adaptation protocol, not data regime: under a matched nine-configuration search (matched search size and selection procedure, not equal compute), fine-tuned CLIP leads ResNet-18 at every measured K (all eight seed-paired contrasts survive Holm–Bonferroni correction within the executed 16-contrast family), whereas frozen probes at default C=1 tie or trail. Validation-selected C narrows the LAION-2B few-shot gap to a non-significant near-tie without making either probe lead; C-selected contrasts are nominal, outside the pre-registered family. We score per-class recall with explicit three-tier weights reflecting asymmetric misdiagnosis cost (viral/virus-vector classes x3, severe fast-acting pathogens x2, remaining classes x1): at the default probe, viral-tier recall is 51.2% for DFN-CLIP versus 74.1% for fine-tuned ResNet-18, a gap that narrows but does not reverse after C selection. For deployment, measured on a desktop GPU, fine-tuned ResNet-18 infers about 34x faster (3,900 vs 116 images/s) with about 3x lower peak memory (1.1 vs 3.5 GB) than CLIP; edge-hardware throughput was not measured. Zero-shot CLIP does not exceed the 13.0% majority-class baseline on TPDD, although the same frozen features reach 86.2% under a supervised probe—a decision-rule mismatch, not a representational ceiling. Tomato and corn checks reproduce only the frozen-probe arm; a 53-image field split gives directional, underpowered evidence of a reversal. The resulting rule is to choose by the binding constraint: fine-tune CLIP (or a 0.19%-parameter LoRA adapter) for accuracy, deploy ResNet-18 for inference cost, and use cached frozen features for iteration—within a single-crop boundary that independent field validation has not yet established.
